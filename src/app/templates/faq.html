Frequently asked questions

How do I write a template with variables?

Write your prompt text and put each part that changes in double braces, for example {{ '{{topic}}' }}. Every different name becomes one variable (up to 10). For each variable you choose a label, a type (Text, Long Text or Number), an optional default value and whether it is required. Names start with a letter or underscore and contain only letters, digits and underscores.

What is the difference between a draft and a published prompt?

A draft is private and only needs a title to be saved. To publish it, the prompt needs a title, a description, a target model, 1 to 5 tags and a template text, and every placeholder must have a variable. If something is missing, all the problems are listed together.

How do versions work?

When you edit a published prompt, your change is saved as a new version (2, 3, ...) with an optional change note. You can open the history and view any version. Rolling back copies an older version into a new latest version, so no version is ever deleted.

What is a fork?

Forking copies someone else's published prompt into your own drafts, with its text, variables, tags and target model. The copy links back to the original, and you can change and publish it as your own. You cannot fork your own prompts.

Does the Workbench send my prompt to an AI model?

No. The Workbench only replaces the placeholders with the values you type and shows the result with its character and word count. You can copy it or download it as .txt, .md or .json. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is saved.

How are reports handled?

You can report a prompt or a review that breaks the community rules, once per item. The owner never sees who reported it. An administrator reviews the report and either dismisses it, hides the content, or suspends the account. Suspended accounts cannot sign in.

Why was I signed out?

For your security, you are signed out after 60 minutes without activity, and on all other devices when you change your password. After 5 wrong passwords in a row, signing in is blocked for 15 minutes.
